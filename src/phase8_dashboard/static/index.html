End-to-End System Architecture (Phases 1 through 8)

Technical blueprint and methodology of the Flight Delay Analytics & Prediction System

1

Phase 1: Data Validation & Quality Audit

Audited 7,079,081 raw records across 12 monthly CSVs. Validated schema uniformity, handled cancelled/diverted flight partitioning, audited null patterns in cause-specific delay fields, and established zero-imputation guardrails.

7.07M RecordsZero Schema DriftIntegrity Verified
2

Phase 2: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

Discovered systemic delay mechanisms: Late inbound aircraft accounts for 40.44% of delay minutes. Established diurnal delay curves (9.6% at 06:00 to 29.4% at 18:00) and seasonal peaks in July (29.62%).

15 Airlines348 Hubs6,805 Routes
3

Phase 3 & 4: Kimball Star Schema Data Warehouse

Engineered an enterprise columnar Star Schema in DuckDB: 1 centralized Fact Table (fact_flights) connected to 5 Dimension Tables (dim_date, dim_carrier, dim_airport, dim_time_block, dim_route) with verified 0 orphaned foreign keys.

Star SchemaDuckDB EngineZero Orphans
5

Phase 5: SQL & OLAP Multidimensional Analytics

Developed complex multidimensional queries utilizing ROLLUP, CUBE, and window ranking. Computed route Pareto concentration (LAX → SFO #1 delay route) and ground taxi friction indexes in 9ms - 20ms.

Rollup & CubeRoute ParetoTaxi Friction
6

Phase 6: Data Mining (Apriori & K-Means)

Discovered 49 high-lift association rules revealing extreme delay multipliers (e.g. CLT/ORD/DFW + Evening + Summer yields Lift > 2.1x). Segmented US airports into 4 operational clusters using K-Means.

Apriori MiningLift > 2.48xK-Means (k=4)
7

Phase 7: Machine Learning Prediction Pipeline

Trained a class-balanced HistGradientBoostingClassifier on strict temporal holdout data (Months 1-10 Train, Months 11-12 Test). Engineered Bayesian smoothed target priors and calibrated optimal decision threshold at 0.337.

HistGradientBoostingROC-AUC 0.6124Threshold 0.337

Phase 8: Neumorphic Interactive Web Platform

Modern Neumorphic interface styled strictly in Ice Silver (#DDE3E8), Deep Aubergine (#3A0F34), and Marigold (#EAAA00). Delivers sub-millisecond inference, live SQL console, and 24-hour best-time-to-fly recommendations.
